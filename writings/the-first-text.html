Dear reader,

it took me a while to realise this and god knows I have been thinking about this letter for days now. At first, I didn’t even notice. I wrote many reincarnations of this letter. I have begun a lot and never finished. I finished a lot but didn’t like the beginnings. I found myself shredding every attempt apart and burning it as urgently as possible. As I was thinking of every sentence, of every word and letter and how they gelled together. I couldn’t stop thinking about you…how hard you are to satisfy and how it’s been impossible to meet your standards. It’s perhaps unclear to you by now but I have become obsessed with you. Perhaps is a weak word to describe how oblivious you can be. The truth is when I noticed it the first time, it made my throat dry knowing I have done it so many times. I wished a lobotomy in the beginning but then I became convinced that it is your standards that blinded me. You subdued me to do it so naturally. But now I feel stupid. It was so obvious, not even hidden under my nose. It is as obvious as a blood spilled onto a dusty floor.

I know I have to be meticulous with your language. I know for you, I need to be magician in my persuasion. And I am aware I emphasised many times already how long it took me to notice it, but you cannot even fathom the how disgusting I felt when I noticed that every time I wrote this letter, I wrote it in English. Although my conscious spoke to me in Arabic, the moment the tip of the pen touched the paper, my words translated without my consent. I felt used. I felt like the eyes of a man staring at my body. Naked even if I weren’t. It wasn’t even my desire write in English. It is as if my subconscious was already on its knees, looking up to you. I felt its hands looked to have been melted together, brazed with the heat of a thousands suns over a thousand and one nights.

But, after saying all of this, and excuse my repetition, I wanted you to know. Fuck it, I needed you to know why now. I know why I would rather take these scissors and cut my own tongue before I could disappoint you. I know now why I keep imagining taking my vocal chords out and offer them if you desired to. I Know, I know, I know, I know…I would rather deflate my own lungs to not even breathe in the wrong for you. If you asked for birds milk in exchange I would have offered. I would carpet the floor in front of each of your steps. It is adorable. I see on TV that my neighbours they already do that.

The truth is, the last few letters I sent you didn’t work out in my favour. If you remember after the 03 letter, I died four million times. And it was over a misspelling. A simple English grammatical mistake. Heck even a few years ago 300 thousand reincarnations of me got taken out because I felt brave enough and dared to speak informally to you in one of the letters.

My poor kids, they are so innocently clueless. They are young enough to look at me and think they will do things differently. They cannot even fathom what could happen to me if I made one mistake in this letter. Something as simple as a wrong word placed in the wrong order, a futile request or a disgusting daring demand would jump start a catastrophe.

I think, breathe and eat in Arabic but yet when I talk to you, when I am in your presence, I am unable to not talk inour language. I want you to know to I get it though. Like get it get it. My bones under whole buildings and rubbles understand you fully. I don’t need any hints anymore. I am tired of them anyway. I understand all the slaps on my hands and all the stern looks. I know what they intend to mean. That is precisely why I want you to know I prepared for this letter. I have done my homework. Reread every word and every symbol. Checked every annunciation and pronunciation. I even went the extra mile, as you like to say, and talked to Sharazad and not to my surprise she talked back to me. She taught me all her tricks and her lessons. She gave me all her advice and opinions. She reminded me of my history and of yours. She showed me truths and the pains that filled it. I used all of that to forge the perfect reply. The perfect rebuke and rebuttal. And I am proud to say I finally found it, I found the perfect answer to solve your problems with me forever. A permanent resolution to what has been a splinter in your finger but a mountain on my head. I would have normally hoped for patience and forgiveness but this time it’s not needed; I can finally stop weeping. My glands have dried anyway;
